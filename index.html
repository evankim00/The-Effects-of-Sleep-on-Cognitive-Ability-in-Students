Where the numbers come from

The model is fitted to measured data from teenagers wherever it exists. Past that, it follows the shape found in adults, and it stops extrapolating where the research stops. D is cumulative sleep debt in hours, n is the number of nights so far, and h is hours slept per night.

  • Research checks: adults kept to 4–6 h in bed for 14 days performed as badly as after 1–2 full nights without sleep6, and their own sleepiness ratings hid how impaired they were.
  • Limits: no controlled study has restricted teens for a whole semester. Real students also sleep in on weekends, drink caffeine and vary widely from person to person.
